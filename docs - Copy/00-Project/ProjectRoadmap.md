# 1. Purpose

This roadmap presents the long-term evolution of ProjectHub.

The roadmap defines product phases, major deliverables and expected outcomes.
It is intended for planning purposes only and may evolve as business priorities change.

---

# 2. Product Vision

ProjectHub will become an integrated Educational ERP platform for training institutes.

The MVP focuses on educational operations.
Future releases will gradually introduce finance, HR and organizational management.

---

# 3. Development Strategy

The project follows an Agile and Incremental approach.

Each phase delivers a usable product increment.

Each Sprint produces:

- Working Software
- Updated Documentation
- Updated Product Backlog

# 7. Guiding Principles

The project follows these principles:

- Business before Technology
- Working Software over Comprehensive Documentation
- Incremental Delivery
- Continuous Refactoring
- Clean Architecture
- Domain-Driven Design
- SOLID Principles
- Testability
- Maintainability

---

# 8. Definition of Success

ProjectHub will be considered successful when:

- Daily educational operations are fully managed through the system.
- Manual processes are minimized.
- Data consistency is maintained.
- The architecture supports future expansion without major redesign.
- New modules can be added independently.

---

# 9. Roadmap Governance

This roadmap is reviewed at the end of each major release.

Changes shall be driven by:

- Business priorities
- Customer feedback
- Sprint Reviews
- Product Owner decisions
