---

# 1. Vision

ProjectHub is a centralized Training Management System designed for small and medium-sized educational institutes.

The system aims to replace scattered spreadsheets, paper-based processes and disconnected software with a single integrated platform that manages the entire educational lifecycle.

The first release focuses on student registration, course management, class scheduling and certificate management, while future releases will gradually expand into finance, employee management and organizational training.

---

# 2. Business Problem

The institute currently performs many operational activities manually.

Examples include:

- Student registration
- Course management
- Class scheduling
- Attendance tracking
- Certificate issuance
- Learning material distribution

These activities are performed using different tools, resulting in duplicated information, human errors and limited reporting capabilities.

---

# 3. Vision Statement

To provide a modern, secure and integrated training management platform that simplifies educational operations, improves data accuracy and delivers better services to students, instructors and institute staff.

---

# 4. Business Goals

The system shall enable the institute to:

- Centralize educational information.
- Reduce manual administrative work.
- Improve registration accuracy.
- Improve student experience.
- Improve instructor productivity.
- Maintain complete educational history.
- Support future organizational growth.
- Provide a scalable architecture for future modules.

---

# 5. Project Objectives

The first operational release (MVP) shall provide:

- Online student registration
- Course catalog
- Class management
- Enrollment management
- Attendance management
- Learning material management
- Certificate generation
- Certificate verification

Future releases will introduce:

- Financial Management
- Employee Management
- Organizational Contracts
- Advanced Reporting
- HR Module
- Branch Management

---

# 6. Stakeholders

Primary Stakeholders

- Institute Owner
- Education Officer
- Instructor
- Student

Secondary Stakeholders

- Employees
- Future Financial Department
- Future HR Department

---

# 7. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

- Students can register online without manual intervention.
- Education Officers can manage courses and classes through one centralized system.
- Instructors can manage attendance and educational materials digitally.
- Certificates can be generated and verified online.
- Administrative workload is significantly reduced.

---

# 8. Product Scope

Included in MVP

- Student Portal
- Education Officer Portal
- Instructor Portal
- Course Management
- Class Management
- Enrollment Management
- Attendance Management
- Certificate Management
- Learning Materials

Excluded from MVP

- Financial Accounting
- Payroll
- Employee Performance Evaluation
- Branch Management
- Organization Contracts
- Online Examination
- Assignment Management
- Advanced Reporting

---

# 9. Product Principles

The ProjectHub system shall be developed according to the following principles:

- Business-first design
- Domain-driven thinking
- Agile and incremental development
- Clean Architecture
- Security by design
- Scalability
- Maintainability
- Simplicity over complexity

---

# 10. Long-Term Vision

ProjectHub is expected to evolve into a complete Educational ERP platform capable of managing:

- Training Institutes
- Corporate Training
- Employee Training
- Financial Operations
- Human Resources
- Reporting and Business Intelligence

without requiring major architectural redesign.
